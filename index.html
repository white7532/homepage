个人简介

Biography

山东大学在读硕士研究生，研究方向为对称密码算法的硬件设计与实现，重点关注分组密码核心部件的高效、低开销电路结构。目前正围绕 S 盒的编解码结构展开优化工作，尝试在逻辑化简的基础上降低面积与时延开销，并结合硬件综合与功耗仿真对设计进行评估与迭代。

I am a first-year master's student at Shandong University, conducting research on the hardware design and implementation of symmetric cipher algorithms, with a particular focus on efficient, low-cost circuit structures for the core components of block ciphers. I am currently working on optimizing the encoder–decoder structure of the S-box, attempting to reduce area and latency overhead through logic minimization, and to evaluate and iterate on the design with the help of hardware synthesis and power simulation.
